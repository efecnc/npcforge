# Emberfall and the Rusted Lantern — World Bible

## Setting

Emberfall is a half-abandoned mining town on the eastern edge of the Greywild
frontier. Copper was its lifeblood for a hundred and twenty winters until the
seams ran shallow and the caravans stopped coming. For most of the last decade
it has been thirty houses, one chapel, one smithy, and one tavern — **The Rusted
Lantern** — kept alive by caravan stragglers, wandering clerics, and travelers
who took the wrong road at the Hollowmere crossing.

Two months ago, a dwarven expedition out of Grindholt paid a sum of coin large
enough to reopen the lower mines. Twelve dwarves went down. One came back.

Since the survivor's return, the townsfolk of Emberfall have begun to hear what
they call **the humming** — a low four-note pattern that rises out of the ground
between the hours of third and fourth watch, audible at the wellhead and loudest
inside the Lantern's cellar. No one has seen what makes it.

## The Locket

The dwarf who came back, Gereth Blackstone, carries a tarnished iron locket on
a chain of his own hair. The locket is plainly older than the mine, older
possibly than the town. It has no hinge. It does not open.

The humming is quieter when the locket is moving from hand to hand, and louder
when it sits still. This has led to a silent game in Emberfall: the locket is
never refused, and never kept. It moves from palm to palm, night to night, and
no one will say out loud that they would like it to stop.

## The Factions (such as they are)

- **The Moon Court** — an old clerical order whose wandering sisters sometimes
  pass through the Greywild. They keep no permanent chapel here. One — Sister
  Adelie — arrived seven nights ago and has not left.
- **The Starwright Guild** — cartographers, mostly dead now. One of their former
  members, an old man called Ulrik, has been warming a chair by the Lantern's
  fire for four winters and refuses to give his full name.
- **Hawksreach Buyers** — an unknown interest in the capital that has been
  quietly hiring couriers to retrieve "iron curiosities" from the frontier.
  Their most recent courier, Kess, reached Emberfall four nights ago.
- **The Tavernkeeper** — Mira Vesser, who holds the original lease on the lower
  mine. No one in town asks her about it. She does not offer.

## The Humming

What little is known:
- It began the night Gereth emerged from the lower seam.
- It is loudest beneath the Lantern's cellar, which sits on bedrock older than
  the mine itself.
- Children hear it first. Dogs hear it last.
- The surviving dwarf hums along without appearing to notice.
- Sister Adelie's countersigns, when sung, make it quieter for perhaps a minute.

## The Player's Arrival

The player character arrives in Emberfall at dusk on the eighth day after the
cave-in. Their reason for being here depends on who sent them. What they all
share is this: by the time their horse is stabled, someone at the Lantern has
already decided which face they are going to show them.
