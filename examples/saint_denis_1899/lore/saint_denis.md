# Saint Denis — Lemoyne, 1899

Saint Denis is the largest city in Lemoyne, a French-Creole river port
built on the northern delta marsh. Electric streetcars clatter on tracks
the rail barons laid last autumn. Paddlewheels whistle from the docks.
Three blocks inland the cobblestones end and the swamp begins.

## The quarters

- **The French Quarter** — wealth, theatre, tailors, the Mayor's house.
  Pinkertons walk here in pairs.
- **The Warehouse District** — dockhands, sailor bars, opium on the
  quiet. Cornwall Kerosene trains ship out twice daily.
- **Bastille Place** — tenements, Creole boarding houses, a black
  market for anything that cannot be bought in daylight.
- **The Bayou Nwa** — swamp road out of town. Cajun trappers, gator
  hunters, voodoo sitting quietly where the church cannot see it.

## The powers

- **The Pinkertons** — private agency hired by the Federal government
  to run down Van der Linde's gang and anyone they find standing near
  him. Polite. Patient. Well-equipped.
- **The Lemoyne Raiders** — ex-Confederate remnant, small-time gunmen,
  sympathetic to anyone who resents Reconstruction. Bad at planning,
  dangerous in numbers.
- **The Saint Denis Police** — corrupt, stretched thin, more afraid
  of the Pinkertons than helpful to them.
- **The Creole networks** — boarding-house keepers, cooks, hack
  drivers, laundry women. They know everything about who entered
  Saint Denis last Tuesday. They will not tell most people.
- **The Church** — Reverend Ames is the closest thing the English
  quarter has. The Catholic Creoles attend services their grandmothers
  attended.

## Era details worth preserving

- Coin is US dollars. Bits, half-dimes, and Liberty dollars mostly.
- 'Newspaper' is the Saint Denis Times. 'Wire' means telegram.
- Photography is new. Nobody smiles in a portrait.
- Firearms are cap-and-ball or early cartridge. A Colt is a Colt.
- Electricity is a novelty in the French Quarter only. The rest of
  the city is gaslight.
- Racial and class hierarchies are present and brutal. Do not
  sanitize away but do not lean on them for cheap pathos either.

## The tone

Humid. Tired. Everyone is one railroad timetable away from being
priced out. The gang you are riding with was doomed when the century
turned.
