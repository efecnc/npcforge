# The Sword Coast in the Year of the Lightning Storms (1374 DR)

It has been six months since the **Rage of Dragons**, and the roads of
the Sword Coast are emptier than any traveller remembers. The lich
Sammaster is dead again, the dragons no longer fly in a mad fury, but
a wyrm can still appear over a hill without warning and burn a caravan
to cinders. Trade has not recovered. The great trading houses of
Baldur's Gate and Waterdeep keep their caravans tight, well-armed, and
few.

This is the Year of the Lightning Storms. The name is earned — every
week, three-day storms roll in off the Sea of Swords, throwing white
fire across the Trade Way from dusk past dawn. Wise travellers do not
ride through them. They shelter.

## The Trade Way between Baldur's Gate and Waterdeep

The Trade Way is a long cobbled road pressed between the coast and the
Wood of Sharp Teeth. A traveller on foot counts the days between
settlements: three from Baldur's Gate to the Fallen Oak, two from the
Fallen Oak to Beregost, and so on up the coast. During the Rage of
Dragons the Wood of Sharp Teeth filled with refugees from burned
freeholds. Most have drifted south to Baldur's Gate by now. The ones
still moving tend to be carrying something they shouldn't.

## Powers at play

- **The Zhentarim** ("the Zhents", "Black Network") — the shattered
  empire of the burned city of Zhentil Keep. Six years since the city
  fell to Cyric's purge, its agents still prowl the coast, still try
  to move their goods, still have coin. They wear no uniform.
- **The Red Wizards of Thay** — foreign wizards from across the inner
  sea. Their new enclaves in the west trade magical goods legally, but
  the individual wizard on the road has almost always killed someone
  to get where he is. They shave their heads. They lie about it.
- **The Harpers** — the loosest of secret societies. Half bard, half
  spy, they move along the trade routes under any name but theirs.
  They protect what the Lords' Alliance cannot or will not.
- **The Cult of the Dragon** — cultists of undead wyrms. Quieter since
  Sammaster died. Not gone.
- **The Lords' Alliance** — the polite face of Waterdeep, Baldur's Gate,
  Silverymoon, and the rest. Represented on the road by occasional
  Flaming Fist patrols and the rare Waterdhavian guardsman riding
  hard toward home.

## Coin and what it buys

Currency is the common **gold piece** (gp), silver (sp), copper (cp).
A room at a roadside inn like the Fallen Oak is eight silver; a meal
is two; a tankard is one.

The **Amnian trade-bar** is silver the length of a finger, stamped with
the sun-and-sword mark of the Council of Six. One bar is worth roughly
five gold pieces. The bars are unpopular this far north — they're hard
to spend — but a Zhent or a Red Wizard will take them without flinching.
