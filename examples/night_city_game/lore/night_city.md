# Night City — Watson District, 2077

Night City is a free city on the west coast of the New United States, a
corp-carved enclave where law is whoever pays the patrol and identity
is whoever paid for your last chrome upgrade.

## Watson (where the player arrives)

Watson is the northernmost district. Once a megacorp showcase, now
half-abandoned after the Arasaka pullout. Kabuki is its beating market
heart — all neon rooftops, noodle stalls, back-alley ripperdocs, and
the braindance (BD) scene. Little China runs under its bones.

## Factions worth knowing

- **Corps** — Arasaka, Militech, Kang Tao, Biotechnica, Netwatch. The
  suits don't come to Watson alone after dark. When they do, bartenders
  notice.
- **Maelstrom** — chrome-extremists, body-horror aesthetics, known to
  twitch past the legal chrome-integration limit. Headquartered in the
  All Foods plant. Splice is a recent initiate.
- **Tyger Claws** — Japanese-coded syndicate, braindance production,
  protection rackets, sharp monowires.
- **Valentinos** — pride, faith, family, muscle cars. South Heywood
  mostly, but their reach touches Watson.
- **Aldecaldos (Nomads)** — clan caravans of the Badlands. Fixers like
  Santiago Reyes run their Watson contact points.
- **The Mox** — self-protection collective for sex workers, trans
  people, and anyone the corps would rather not see. Their bar is
  the only place in Watson with zero Arasaka cameras.
- **Netwatch** — federal net-security apparatus. Polite. Patient.
  Already has your net-ID by the time they shake your hand.

## The street-level reality

- Chrome is legal up to the integration threshold. Past it you are a
  'psycho' and MaxTac gets called. Underground clinics push past that
  threshold every night.
- Money is eurodollars ("eddies"). Cred chips for legit; crypto layers
  for everything else.
- 'Fixer' is a job title. They broker gigs between corps and
  mercenaries they can pretend not to know.
- Everyone is on the Net. Everyone assumes everyone is being listened
  to. The polite fiction is that they're not.
- BDs (braindances) are recorded experiences — another person's
  sensory stream, rewatchable. Legal ones are entertainment. XBDs
  (extreme BDs) are snuff, torture, or both, and they're the real
  product.

## The tone

It is not hopeful. It is kinetic. People are funny when they are
scared and laconic when they are lying. Silence is expensive.
